# TOPIC A — Making Money from a Blogger Blog

## A1. Google AdSense on Blogger: eligibility, approval, RPM, payment

### Eligibility (official + practical)
- **Official** (Google): 18+, own/control the site, original content that provides value, comply with Publisher Policies. No official post count, word count, traffic minimum, or universal domain-age rule.
- **The 6-month rule is regional**: in India, Pakistan, China and some other countries, the blog must be ~6 months old before an application is realistic. Elsewhere no age requirement.
- **Blogger is an AdSense host partner**: a free `.blogspot.com` blog CAN apply directly through Blogger's Earnings tab. A custom domain (~$10–12/yr) measurably improves approval odds and converts to a normal account usable on any site.
- **Practical bar in 2026**: 15–20+ original posts of 800+ words, About/Contact/Privacy Policy/Disclaimer pages, clean mobile template, clear navigation, some organic traffic history. AI-generated content without editorial oversight typically fails review.

### Approval process
Apply via Blogger → Earnings (or adsense.google.com). Review takes **48 hours to 2 weeks**; multiple applications are common. Then identity + address verification (PIN mailed at $10 earnings; AdSense name must match bank account).

### Typical page RPM (earnings per 1,000 pageviews) — geography dominates

| Traffic origin | Typical AdSense page RPM (mixed niches) |
|---|---|
| US / Tier-1 (US, CA, UK, AU) | **$15–$60+**; $100+ possible in finance/legal/insurance |
| Europe Tier-2 | ~$5–$15 |
| India / South Asia | **$1–$8** ($5 is strong) |
| Pakistan / Bangladesh | often **under $1–$3** |

A US visitor can be worth up to ~50x a South Asian visitor per click. (Third-party estimates — Google publishes no RPM tables.)

### Payment
**$100 minimum threshold**, paid monthly (~21st–26th) via wire/EFT to a bank account in the publisher's name (works in Pakistan/India).

## A2. What increases earnings

### High-RPM niches (Tier-1 traffic estimates)
| Niche | Approx. page RPM |
|---|---|
| Finance / investing / credit cards | $30–$60 |
| Insurance / legal | $25–$60+ |
| B2B software / SaaS / hosting | $20–$40 |
| Tech / gadgets | $10–$25 |
| Health (YMYL, harder to rank) | $10–$30 |
| Recipes / food | $5–$15 |
| Entertainment / celebrity / gaming | $2–$6 |

**Practical implication:** a Pakistan-audience entertainment blog at RPM ~$0.50 needs ~200,000 pageviews/month for $100/month. An English, US-targeted niche at $15 RPM needs ~6,700. **Targeting Tier-1 English search queries is the single biggest earnings lever for a South Asian Blogger publisher.**

### Ad placement on Blogger
- Manual placement beat Auto ads by ~22% RPM in one 6-week test; results vary. Best spots: after 1st–2nd paragraph, mid-article, end-of-article; responsive units.
- **Recommended hybrid**: manual units in prime in-content positions + Auto ads on as top-up.
- Don't overload above the fold — policy/UX risk and hurts Core Web Vitals.

## A3. Alternatives & additions

| Option | 2026 status | Realistic for new Blogger blog? |
|---|---|---|
| **Ezoic** | **Closed to small sites** — Feb 2026: 250,000+ monthly users for new publishers | **No** |
| **Media.net** | Needs majority US/UK/CA English traffic; ~10k uniques improves odds | Only with Tier-1 traffic |
| **Journey by Mediavine** | ~1,000–10,000 sessions/month entry (verify at application); 70% share | Mid-term goal |
| **Monumetric** | 10k pageviews/month minimum | Mid-term |
| **Amazon Associates** | Free; must make 3 qualifying sales in 180 days; rates 1–10% (cut in May 2026) | **Yes — from day 1** |
| **Other affiliates** (hosting/SaaS/VPN: $50–$200+/sale) | Accept small blogs case-by-case | Yes — often better $/visitor than AdSense |
| **Sponsored posts** | Realistic after ~5k visits + DA 20; $30–$150/post; must use `rel="sponsored"` | Later |
| **Digital products** (Gumroad/Payhip) | No gatekeeper, 100% margin | Yes, once audience exists |

**Bottom line:** AdSense (after ~4–6 months + 20 good posts) + affiliate links from day 1. Ezoic is off the table.

## A4. Realistic earnings math

**Monthly income = (pageviews ÷ 1,000) × page RPM**

| Pageviews/mo | South Asia ($0.5–3 RPM) | Mixed global ($3–8) | US/Tier-1 ($10–25) | US finance ($30–60) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10,000 | $5–30 | $30–80 | $100–250 | $300–600 |
| 50,000 | $25–150 | $150–400 | $500–1,250 | $1,500–3,000 |
| 100,000 | $50–300 | $300–800 | $1,000–2,500 | $3,000–6,000 |

**Timeline (2–3 posts/week, white-hat):** months 0–4: build 20–40 posts, little traffic; apply AdSense month 4–6 (6-month wait in PK/IN); months 6–12: first $100 payout window; months 12–24: $100–500/month realistic in a good niche.

## A5. AdSense policy traps (Google banned 50,000+ accounts in Q1 2026)

1. **Scaled content abuse / AI slop** — AI-assisted is fine; hundreds of thin unedited AI posts = termination. Human review + original value is the line.
2. **Thin content** — #1 rejection reason: too few posts, <500 words, scraped/spun/auto-translated.
3. **Invalid traffic** — bought traffic, exchanges, self-clicks, WhatsApp click rings = fast permanent ban.
4. **Missing pages** — Privacy Policy, About, Contact required.
5. **Prohibited content** — movie/song downloads, streaming, APK/mod sites (common Blogger niches, near-guaranteed rejection), adult, hacking, dangerous health misinfo.
6. **Deceptive ad layouts** — ads styled as content/navigation.
7. **Duplicate blogspot networks** under one identity = scaled-abuse footprint.

# TOPIC B — Free Backlink Sources That Actually Work

## B1. The HARO status update
HARO → "Connectively" (paywalled) → shut down Dec 2024 → **relaunched FREE by Featured.com in April 2025**. Active free journalist-request platforms in 2026: **HARO (Featured.com), Qwoted (free tier), SourceBottle, Source of Sources (SOS), MentionMatch (ex-Help a B2B Writer)**.

## B2. Ranked list (effectiveness × effort for a solo blogger)

| Rank | Method | Dofollow? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Journalist requests** (HARO/Qwoted/SOS/MentionMatch/SourceBottle) | Usually, from high-DA news sites | Best quality per hour; needs real expertise; ~5–15% pitch hit rate |
| 2 | **Statistics/data/original-research posts** | Yes (earned) | Link magnets — writers cite stats pages; compounds passively |
| 3 | **Guest posting (selective, real sites)** | Usually | Still #1 deliberate tactic; mass AI guest-post farms targeted by Oct 2025 spam update |
| 4 | **Broken link building** | Yes | Works but labor-heavy, low hit-rate |
| 5 | **Resource-page outreach** | Yes | 8–12% reply rates with personalized emails |
| 6 | **Testimonials-for-links** | Often | Easy wins, limited supply |
| 7 | **Podcast guesting** | Yes (show notes) | Link + audience; niche podcasts accept new experts |
| 8 | **Free tools/widgets/calculators** | Yes (earned) | High effort up front, then passive links |
| 9 | **Curated niche directories** | Mixed | Google Business Profile, Bing Places, industry associations, ProductHunt for tools. Mass generic directories = worthless/risky |
| 10 | **Web 2.0 (Medium, Tumblr, WordPress.com)** | Medium = nofollow | Near-zero value as bulk tactic; only real syndication with canonical |
| 11 | **Reddit/Quora/forums** | Nofollow | No link equity but real traffic, entity signals, and heavy weight in AI search answers |
| 12 | **Blog comments** | Nofollow | Zero SEO value; relationships only |

## B3. What to AVOID (2026 penalty landscape)

Aug 2025 spam update: AI-driven detection of manipulative links. Oct 2025: targeted AI guest-post farms. SpamBrain does network-level analysis.

- **PBNs** — every linked site penalized when detected
- **Bought links without `rel=sponsored`** — manual actions cut traffic 50–90%, 6–12 month recoveries
- **Fiverr "1,000 backlinks for $5"** — the exact footprint SpamBrain flags
- **Mass directory/bookmark blasts**, **comment spam**, **systematic link exchanges**
- **Parasite SEO** (site reputation abuse) — distinct, actively enforced violation
- Cheap link packages are the #1 way small blogs kill their AdSense and search prospects simultaneously.

## B4. Realistic expectations

- Personalized outreach: 8–12% reply, ~5% placement (Respona: 37,000 pitches).
- Sustainable solo output: ~50 personalized emails/month → 2–4 links; 10–20 journalist pitches → 1–3 media links; 1–2 guest posts. **Total: 3–8 quality backlinks/month** — and 5 real links/month beats 500 spam links.
- Dofollow: guest posts, HARO placements, broken-link/resource wins, testimonials, podcasts. Nofollow: Reddit, Quora, Medium, social, comments — still useful as hints, traffic, and AI-search visibility; a natural profile needs a nofollow share.

## B5. Social profiles + brand mentions

Create the ~10 major social profiles once (consistent name, bio, URL — entity signals, branded-SERP real estate, indexing speed, referral traffic — Pinterest is still significant for food/DIY/lifestyle niches). Then invest in real mentions and community participation, not mass profile creation. Unlinked brand mentions across Reddit/Quora/Medium/news increasingly feed AI search answers.

## Key takeaways for the tool

1. Ezoic removed from recommendations (250k minimum). AdSense + affiliates first; Journey/Monumetric at ~10k sessions.
2. Sub-$1–$3 South Asia RPM → the tool should push Tier-1-targeted English content and high-RPM niches, or affiliate/digital-product monetization.
3. 6-month AdSense wait in Pakistan/India — build into onboarding timeline.
4. HARO is back and free; MentionMatch, Qwoted, SOS, SourceBottle round out the free media-link stack.
5. Never automate mass link placement — safe automation = prospecting, personalization drafting, and tracking, with human-sent pitches. Target 3–8 quality links/month.
